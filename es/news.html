Discovery stays on the hub; payments stay on Litbuy

litbuysspreadsheets.com and the MaisonLooks hub never touch your card. If any page asks for payment outside litbuy.com, stop and verify the URL. Copy marketplace links from the sheet, then paste into Litbuy’s order form—same flow as other Taobao / Weidian agents.

Warehouse QC is the binding photo set

Thumbnails in a row can be old seller shots. The photos that decide whether you ship or return are the ones Litbuy takes after the item hits their warehouse. Use spreadsheet QC as a preview only—approve or reject from the warehouse album.
