Litbuy spreadsheet FAQ

Short answers for long-tail searches—litbuy spreadsheet, Taobao / Weidian / 1688 links, warehouse QC, consolidation, and what this hub does versus Litbuy checkout.

What does “Litbuy spreadsheet” mean on this site?

Our merchant-curated browsing hub: marketplace links (Taobao, Weidian, 1688) with batch notes and QC hints. You shortlist and compare here, then paste a chosen link into Litbuy to place your order.

Is this the official Litbuy spreadsheet?

No. We are Litbuy merchants. Policies, billing, and support tickets belong on Litbuy’s own domains and apps.

Where do I pay, top up my Litbuy balance, and approve warehouse QC photos?

Only inside Litbuy. This domain never collects card details, wallet top-ups, service fees, or order funds.

How should I read Taobao, Weidian, or 1688 QC in a merchant spreadsheet versus Litbuy warehouse inspection photos?

Treat spreadsheet and seller QC as orientation. The binding check is what Litbuy photographs after the item reaches its warehouse—approve or reject from those images before international shipping.

Why compare similar batch links in a Litbuy spreadsheet before pasting the final URL into Litbuy?

Comparable listings can differ by factory batch, price swing, or photo history. The sheet groups those links so you can pick the strongest URL, then submit that single link to Litbuy.

How do I order an item I found in a Litbuy spreadsheet—where do I paste the Taobao or Weidian link?

Copy the marketplace URL from the spreadsheet row, open Litbuy’s item or link submission flow, and paste it there. This site does not create carts or checkouts—Litbuy owns purchase steps end to end.

What if a spreadsheet link is dead, shows a 404, or the seller removed the listing?

Marketplace sellers delist without notice. Compare neighboring rows for alternates, ask merchant community channels if you are stuck, then submit a working URL to Litbuy once you have one.

Does litbuysspreadsheets.com handle Litbuy agent fees, storage, or international shipping invoices?

No. Agent fee schedules, warehouse storage timers, rehearsal packing, and final shipping invoices are configured and paid only inside Litbuy after you submit links from the hub you trust.

Can I combine multiple spreadsheet picks into one international parcel on Litbuy?

Yes, when Litbuy’s consolidation tools allow it. After each item clears warehouse QC, you typically group parcels or submit a combined ship request per Litbuy’s current shipping UI—this spreadsheet site cannot merge orders for you.

Who handles customs, duties, and import paperwork after I ship with Litbuy?

Import rules depend on your destination country and the shipping line you pick inside Litbuy. This marketing page cannot declare values or pay duties—you follow Litbuy’s guidance and your local regulations at checkout and delivery.
